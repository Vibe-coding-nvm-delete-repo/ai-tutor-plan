Short answer

Yes, online is usually cheaper. No travel, no room cost, one tutor can serve any city. College test human help runs about $100-300 per hour in 2026. Top names charge $200-500 per hour. Group video classes run $500-1,500 total. Self-paced video runs $129-397. Law test human help runs about $100-300 per hour, top tutors $500-1,000 per hour. Monthly AI-led courses run $69-179 per month. General AI tutor runs $4 per month. Free official practice exists for both tests.

What we will sell

A virtual AI tutor that acts like a strict coach, not a video library. It finds your repeat mistake pattern, gives you a daily plan tied to your test date, and predicts your score.

Start with reading and writing because short passages plus clear rules are the fastest to tutor well. Add math next with the built-in graphing calculator. Tutors are skilled remote staff, hired on score and teaching proof, shown with real names and real voices. No fake identities, ever. Trust is the product.

D. Verdict: college test first, for value and volume

College first. Over 2M takers per year and parents already pay $1,500-6,000 per prep cycle, so value pricing lands: $59 self-serve, $199 with a monthly human check, $649-849 small groups. Start with reading and writing, add math next. Remote tutors hired on score and proof keep cost low and margin high, sold honestly on results with real names and voices.

Law later. Same engine ports cleanly to a pure-text test with adult buyers and ~$230 per hour tutor rates. It becomes tier-two expansion once the college loop prints.

E. Optimal price play if we are better in every way

  • Beat free, do not join it. Free practice and $4 generic AI exist, so Level 1 wins on plan plus trap finder plus score predictor, not on question count. $59 sits under the $129-397 rival band while feeling premium next to $4 generic.
  • Mid tier is the trust tier. Rivals sell single human hours at $200-300. At $199 per month with daily AI plus one reviewed human call, the buyer gets a month of coaching for less than one hour elsewhere. Offer $499 per 3 months to lock in full cycles.
  • High tier caps human cost. Rival 1-on-1 packs run $2,000-6,000. At $649-849 per month for small groups of 6-8 with the same teacher, results stay close while cost per buyer drops hard. Keep groups full or pause intake.
  • Copy what buyers already expect. Monthly cancel-anytime, yearly at ~20% off, 7-day $1 trial or free 5-minute score check to start, promise only with clear work rules so refunds stay capped.
  • Charge for value, show the math. Publish the parent view: hours worked, predicted lift, cost per point vs a $250 human hour. Remote staff paid above local market keeps margin near 70-80% on human tiers without hiding who teaches.

Watch these costs

  • Human time is the limit. Keep reviews to 30 min with pre-read notes. Keep groups full at 6-8.
  • AI cost per heavy user. Cap full-test grading and long chats in Level 1.
  • Refunds. Tight promise rules or refunds eat margin.
  • Pakistan pay reality (verified Sept 2026): top-rated marketplace SAT tutors ask $15-25/hr; full-time remote runs PKR 30-100k/mo ($108-360). Pay above that and margin still holds.
  • Timezone trap: US East Coast evenings are 3-8am in Pakistan. Staff early-morning shifts with a differential or sell US morning and weekend slots, or churn eats you.
